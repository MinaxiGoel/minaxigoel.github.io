A/B TEST · VWO · CRO · SESSION RECORDINGS

Improving form conversion through multi-step optimization

EndNote contact journey. The key decision was not only whether the variation won, but how confidently the team should interpret the signal.

Problem I Solved

A long embedded contact form created friction during a high-value conversion journey. The team needed evidence on whether splitting it into two steps would increase submissions without worsening the experience.

My Approach

I shaped the hypothesis, defined success metrics, reviewed VWO outputs, and compared conversion trends with 20 session recordings to understand both conversion movement and user effort.

Outcome And Impact

The variation showed a 12.62% expected uplift, moving conversion from 4.10% to 4.62%, with completion time improving from 3m29s to 1m52s. I clearly flagged the 66.76% confidence caveat.

BEHAVIORAL ANALYTICS · GA4 · VWO · ROADMAP INPUT

Building a campaign measurement framework for optimization decisions

Tier-1 campaign pages needed a repeatable way to measure behavior after launch and compare signals across months and campaigns.

Problem I Solved

Campaign pages were being reviewed as one-off launches. Teams lacked a consistent longitudinal baseline to separate surface-level traffic movement from deeper engagement and navigation behavior.

My Approach

I created a repeatable observation model across 1st, 3rd, 6th, and 12th month snapshots using heat maps, scroll maps, click maps, attention maps, session recordings, and GA4 metrics.

Outcome And Impact

The framework exposed hidden pathway friction: Top 100 views rose 57% and sessions rose 67%, while listing page views fell 20% and methodology views rose 89%. Findings shaped CTA and content-priority recommendations.

BRAND RESEARCH · AI SEARCH · COMPETITIVE INTELLIGENCE

Researching AI-era brand discovery and competitor positioning

Leadership needed a clearer evidence base for how competitors explain their value and how AI search changes brand discovery.

Problem I Solved

Competitor narratives around trust, rigor, innovation, and AI were difficult to compare. The team needed a structured view of positioning patterns, white spaces, and AI-search implications.

My Approach

I compared 7 corporate narrative competitors across identity, values, and AI/innovation posture, then paired that review with AI SEO, AEO, and GEO research to identify practical content and measurement implications.

Outcome And Impact

The work gave stakeholders an executive-ready framework for messaging, structured proof points, comparison content, FAQs, AI visibility tracking, and clearer brand accuracy measurement.
