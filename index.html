A/B TESTING · VWO · CRO · SESSION RECORDINGS

Reduced EndNote Contact Sales form drop-off with experimentation

Tested form structure as a conversion lever, connecting CRO experimentation, behavioral evidence, and lead generation impact.

Context

The EndNote Contact Sales form was a high-value lead path where completion behavior directly affected commercial opportunity.

Problem to Solve

Users were dropping off before completing a lengthy form, creating friction in a critical conversion journey and limiting lead volume.

Approach

Ran a VWO A/B test comparing single-step vs. multi-step designs, then reviewed session recordings to interpret user effort and completion behavior.

Impact & Outcome

Delivered a 12.6% uplift in submissions and 46.41% faster completion, showing that reduced visible field load lowered cognitive effort.

BEHAVIORAL ANALYTICS · MICROSOFT CLARITY · VWO · HEATMAPS

Built behavioral baselines for 4 annual Tier-1 campaigns

Turned campaign measurement from one-off reporting into a repeatable optimization system for year-over-year decision-making.

Context

The framework covered 4 annual Tier-1 campaigns: Citation Laureates, Highly Cited Researchers, Top 100 Innovators, and Drugs to Watch.

Problem to Solve

No behavioral baselines existed, making year-over-year comparison, campaign learning, and optimization prioritization difficult.

Approach

Built a longitudinal framework tracking 1st, 3rd, and 6th month engagement trends with Microsoft Clarity, heatmaps, scrollmaps, and recordings.

Impact & Outcome

Established performance benchmarks, enabled longitudinal behavioral comparison, and informed evidence-backed priorities for future campaign optimization.
