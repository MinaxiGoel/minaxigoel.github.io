CLARIVATE · A/B TESTING · VWO · CRO · SESSION RECORDINGS

Reduced EndNote Contact Sales form drop-off with experimentation

Tested form structure as a conversion lever, connecting CRO experimentation, behavioral evidence, and lead generation impact.

The Problem

  • Users were dropping off before completing the lengthy EndNote Contact Sales form, limiting high-value lead generation.
  • The team needed evidence on whether reducing visible form load could lift form completion without adding confusion.

Research in Action

  • Designed a VWO A/B test comparing the single-step control against a multi-step variation.
  • Used session recordings to interpret completion behavior beyond the conversion metric.

Actionable Insight

  • The multi-step design made the form feel shorter by reducing the number of visible fields.
  • Average completion moved from 3m29s to 1m52s, signaling lower cognitive effort.

Outcome

  • +12.6% submissions from the optimized experience.
  • 46.41% faster completion, creating evidence to scale form optimization decisions.

CLARIVATE · VOICE OF CUSTOMER · NPS · KEY DRIVER ANALYSIS · THEMATIC ANALYSIS

Executed Annual Voice of Customer program to improve NPS and reveal innovation opportunities

Used customer feedback to identify experience priorities, support retention and advocacy, and convert VOC signals into data-driven action.

The Problem

  • The organization needed a stronger signal on customer needs to improve experience, retention, advocacy, and perceived brand value.
  • Low participation limited confidence in data-driven decisions and prioritization of experience drivers.

Research in Action

  • Measured drivers across purchase, product quality, functionality, innovation, service, education, and communication.
  • Expanded capture through QR codes, events, web, social, and email, then applied key-driver and thematic analysis.

Actionable Insight

  • Key-driver analysis revealed communication, innovation, product functionality, and product quality as important but underperforming drivers.
  • The findings gave teams clear priorities to close the loop with detractors and convert VOC signals into roadmap action.

Outcome

  • Increased NPS by 3 points in 2025.
  • Increased responses by 24.7% to 19,667 and raised response rate to 2%.

MAGICBRICKS · RAG LLM · HUGGING FACE · GOOGLE COLAB · DOCUMENT AI

RERA document chatbot POC

Created a working Python-based RAG prototype to help users query complex property documents and evaluate legal, project, and builder-risk signals.

The Problem

  • Property buyers had to inspect hundreds of RERA documents to find project-specific answers.
  • Critical legal, builder-reputation, and investment-risk information was buried across handwritten, multilingual, typed, and sketched files.

Research in Action

  • Coded the prototype in Python on Google Colab and connected a RAG LLM using Hugging Face tokens.
  • Connected Google Sheets for inputs and Google Drive for outputs; used ChatGPT only to refine implementation code.

Actionable Insight

  • A query-first chatbot reframed document review from manual search to natural-language investigation.
  • Retrieval-augmented generation helped scan project documents and return targeted answers for confident property decisions.

Outcome

  • Delivered a functioning Python-based chatbot POC that accepted project-specific queries and returned document-grounded responses.
  • Demonstrated how RAG could reduce effort in legal and reputation checks for high-stakes investment decisions.

IIIT HYDERABAD · MASTER'S THESIS · VR · QUANTITATIVE RESEARCH

Psycho-motor Correlates of Affective Experience

Led a master's thesis study on how head-scanning behavior in virtual reality can complement emotion assessment with objective behavioral signals.

The Problem

  • Affective experience is subjective and difficult to measure through self-report alone.
  • The study needed to connect psycho-motor behavior in VR environments with measurable affective-response signals.

Research in Action

  • Served as team lead for a 6-member master's thesis project at IIIT Hyderabad.
  • Compared yaw-based head-scanning behavior with valence ratings using quantitative correlation analysis.

Actionable Insight

  • Head movement patterns can add behavioral context to how users experience immersive content.
  • Objective measures strengthen interpretation when self-reported emotion data is incomplete or biased.

Outcome

  • Found significant positive correlations with valence: yaw SD r(118)=0.223, p=0.015 and yaw scanning speed r(118)=0.27, p=0.03.
  • Established head-scanning behavior as a measurable signal for affective experience in VR research.
